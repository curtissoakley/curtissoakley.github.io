About Me

To bolster my programming skills, I decided to learn a bit of web development. For most of the websites lifespan, I imagine it will look rather rough. I took no courses on this in college and don't need these skills for my job. Those two facts are part of the reason I've chosen this as a side-project. Websites are also something tangible and relatable when I discuss it with folks. I've used AI as minimally as possible to maintain the fact that I can explain each aspect of the website design. In my estimation, if this become untrue then how much did I really learn here. The current objective of this website is to host a sports schedule for me of all my teams, formatted in a nice calendar view so I can see who my teams play. I imagine it'll baloon into other ideas as I have them and hopefully can use it as a displaycase for self-improvement.
